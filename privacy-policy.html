Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Artist Level Up (“Artist Level Up”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use https://artistlevelup.com.ng and related Artist Level Up services, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: information you provide when registering or managing an account, such as your name, username, email address and profile details.
  • Creator content: music, beats, artwork, photographs, videos, descriptions and other material you choose to upload or publish.
  • Transactions: order details, payment status, payout details and transaction references needed to operate purchases, marketplace features and creator payouts. Payment card or bank credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider; do not send them to us in support messages.
  • Connected services: if you choose to connect an external account, we receive only the information and permissions made available through the authorization you approve. For Google or YouTube connections, this may include basic account/channel information and the specific read-only data needed to verify the connected channel.
  • Technical and usage information: device, browser, log, security and service-use information that may be processed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the platform.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact us.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; provide artist, creator and marketplace features; display content you choose to publish; process and reconcile transactions; verify connected services you request; provide support; maintain security; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with legal obligations; and improve the reliability of the platform.

3. Google and YouTube data

If you authorize a Google or YouTube connection, Artist Level Up uses the granted permissions only to provide the feature you requested, such as identifying and verifying the YouTube channel associated with your account. We do not use Google user data to serve advertising, sell it, or create advertising profiles. We do not share Google user data with third parties except as needed to provide the requested feature, comply with law, protect the service, or as otherwise permitted by Google's applicable policies and your authorization.

Google access can be revoked at any time through your Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future access through that authorization. Information already received may be retained only as needed for the purposes described in this policy, security, dispute handling or legal compliance, and will be deleted when no longer reasonably required.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us host and secure the platform, process payments, deliver communications, or provide a feature you request. These providers may process information only for the relevant service. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, property or platform security.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your password and account access secure and should never share passwords, PINs or one-time codes.

6. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal requirements and protect the platform. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You may update available profile information through your account, disconnect an external service through that service's account settings, and contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information. We may need to retain some records where required by law or for legitimate security, accounting or dispute purposes.

8. Children

Artist Level Up is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.
