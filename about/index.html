ABOUT ARTIST LEVEL UP

A music discovery and growth platform for independent artists

Artist Level Up (ALU) is a digital music platform built to connect independent musicians with listeners and bring useful creator tools into one place. Singers, rappers, songwriters and music producers can build their presence, share their work, discover opportunities and develop their music journey.

Listeners can explore emerging talent, discover songs and artists, browse music genres and enjoy creator content. Our goal is to make it easier for independent music and the people behind it to be discovered.

Why use Artist Level Up?

  • Bring your artist identity and music presence together in one platform.
  • Give listeners more ways to discover your songs, profile and creative content.
  • Connect music creation, artist promotion and music discovery in one experience.
  • Help independent singers, rappers, songwriters and producers showcase what they do.
  • Explore a beat marketplace where producers can present instrumentals and licensing choices.

Who is Artist Level Up for?

Artists: Independent singers, rappers, songwriters, beat makers and music producers who want to build a profile and share their creativity.

Listeners: Music fans who want to discover emerging artists, explore new sounds and follow creative content.

Producers and beat makers: Creators who want to showcase instrumentals and make their beat listings discoverable.

Frequently asked questions

What is Artist Level Up?

Artist Level Up is a music discovery and artist-growth platform that brings artist profiles, music discovery, creator tools, visual content and a beat marketplace together.

Who can join Artist Level Up?

Independent artists, singers, rappers, songwriters, producers and music listeners can create an account and use the parts of the platform available to them.

Can music fans discover new artists?

Yes. The platform includes artist and song discovery, trending and new-release areas, featured artists and genre browsing.

Does Artist Level Up guarantee streams, fame or income?

No. Artist Level Up provides discovery and creator tools, but it does not guarantee streams, audience growth, fame or earnings. Results depend on many factors, including the artist's work and audience response.
